# Satonaka

Founder and architect of the **M5 Ecosystem** and the **Sovereign Internet**—building human-authorized infrastructure for identity, value, AI, and women-led enterprise.

## What is behind the private M5 Ecosystem repository?

The private **M5Ecosystem** repository is the protected orchestration and development environment for an integrated body of M5 infrastructure. It coordinates work across:

- human authority, identity, permissions, and verifiable records;
- local-first AI, agents, and member-controlled digital environments;
- sovereign economic, commerce, and operational workflows;
- open standards, reference implementations, and developer tooling;
- education, contributor systems, trainer capacity, and venture activation; and
- the infrastructure supporting the path from the First 100 toward 100,000 women-led ventures.

The repository is private by design. It contains active development, security-sensitive implementation details, pre-release systems, internal operations, and protected contributor work. Private does not mean closed in purpose: public-interest standards, documentation, open-source components, and reference materials are released through the appropriate M5 and TitleChain Foundation repositories when they are ready for public use and review.

Access to private repositories is limited to authorized contributors and partners with an active role and appropriate permissions. Public contribution pathways are provided through the organizations and repositories above.
